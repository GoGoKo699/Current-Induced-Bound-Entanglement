# Scope and open questions

- **Resolved transition operators only.** Distinct population-transfer terms use rank-one jumps `sqrt(Q_ij)|i><j|`. Generic degeneracy-mixed Davies jump operators can couple off-diagonal sectors.
- **Hermitian diagonal dephasing.** Gaussian nonnegative factorization relies on the real diagonal noise vectors; arbitrary complex diagonal dissipators are not proved covered.
- **Faithful reversibility.** A positive stationary detailed-balance vector is assumed. Reducible models may never reach PPT/EB at finite time; the all-time equivalence still holds.
- **Circulation family, not universal current law.** The proof of PPT-bound entanglement for all nonzero bias is specific to the three-level cycle and `0<=gamma<=1/2`.
- **Unknown exact biased EB onset.** Realignment `R>1` gives a sufficient entanglement witness and therefore a lower bound for `t_EB`, not its exact location. No upper/lower matching time law is established away from equilibrium.
- **No longer memory lifetime demonstrated.** The observed effect is earlier disappearance of distillability while entanglement survives, with relaxation singular values held fixed.
- **No capacity-rate formula.** Only the sign of two-way assisted quantum capacity is identified. Other communication capacities and private-key functionality remain unassessed.
- **Scientific provenance.** The existing matrix-cone classification, CLDUI/PCP techniques, and realignment witnesses are foundational prior art, not claimed as new. The literature search is targeted rather than exhaustive, and scientific correctness is not certified by numerical agreement alone.
